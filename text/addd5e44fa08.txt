FAQs
What is the experience like for candidates? Is it free for them?

Hirevue is free for candidates, and there are no app downloads required! Candidates love the flexibility of interviewing anytime, anywhere. The result? Candidates report an average NPS (net promoter score) of 71.

Do you provide training?

Yes! Our best-in-class professional services team will be your change management partners. We ensure your hiring team is equipped to make the most of your investment and achieve a faster, fairer, friendlier hiring process.

Do you integrate with my ATS?

We have deep integrations with the leading ATS and CRM technology companies in the HCM space and the ability to integrate with all leading ATS systems.

What security standards do you meet?

Hirevue meets rigorous security standards such as SOC 2 Type 2, ISO/IEC 27001:2013 and is fully GDPR compliant. We are also the only virtual interviewing and assessment provider FedRAMP Certified.

What is your customer support access like?

We have 24/7 customer support in 20+ different languages. You can rest assured your candidates and hiring team are getting the support they need.
