What's included in the free trial?
---
Your trial includes 75 credits and access to most features in the plan you selected.
---
Some trial-specific limits still apply, and available email-provider connections may depend on the plan and trial setup.
---
What happens when my trial ends?
---
You can upgrade to a paid plan to keep full access, or move to our free Starter plan — which stays free with no time limit.
---
Credits are pooled across your entire team and granted upfront at the start of each billing cycle, so your full allotment is available from day one.
---
Learn more about what features consume credits in our knowledge base article or what these actions charge in credits in your Apollo account.
---
Credits reset at the start of each new billing cycle and do not roll over if unused. You can monitor your balance at any time by following these steps.
---
Do unused credits roll over?
---
No. Unused credits expire at the end of each billing cycle and do not carry over. This applies to both plan-included credits and purchased add-on credits.
---
How can I buy more credits if I run out?
---
Go to Settings → Manage Subscription → Customize Credits and use the slider to add extra credits for your current billing cycle.
---
Add-on credits are charged immediately when added.
---
Add-on credits remain available until the end of the current billing period even if you downgrade before then.
---
Monthly vs. annual billing — what's the difference?
---
Monthly billing — Pay month-to-month, cancel anytime. Higher effective per-month cost.
