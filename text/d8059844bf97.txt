Frequently asked questions
How do I get started?

Sign up on the Start plan and you get two credits plus an inbound number valued at $15/month, with no card required.

How does Bland's pricing compare to platforms like Vapi or Retell?

Their advertised per-minute rate is the platform fee only. Vapi lists $0.05/min and Retell lists about $0.07/min for their voice infrastructure. You then pay separately for the LLM, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and telephony. Adding typical provider rates (a leading LLM, Deepgram or equivalent STT, ElevenLabs or OpenAI TTS, and Twilio) brings most production stacks to roughly $0.13 to $0.30 per minute on Vapi and $0.11 to $0.25 per minute on Retell, depending on which model and voice you pick. Bland's per-minute rate covers the LLM, STT, and TTS in one number, with no token charges and no model-provider pass-throughs. Telephony is billed separately, on your own carrier or Bland's at pass-through cost.

Can we bring our own telephony provider?

Yes. Bland integrates with your existing Twilio account, with SIP trunks from any contact-center or telephony provider, or you can use Bland's built-in Twilio at pass-through cost. BYOT customers do not pay transfer fees. You handle carrier costs directly and Bland charges only the per-minute AI rate.

How fast can we go live?

Most teams deploy a first agent within a day on the self-serve plans. Enterprise deployments with custom integrations, number porting, and compliance review follow our 28-day deployment framework: scope, build, gray/red/green-team test, and go live with our forward-deployed engineering team alongside yours.

Is Bland HIPAA, SOC 2, GDPR, and PCI compliant?

Yes. SOC 2 Type I and Type II, HIPAA-eligible with a signed BAA, GDPR, and PCI DSS. Compliance documentation is available under NDA on Enterprise.

Who runs the implementation?

Our forward-deployed engineering team works directly with yours to build, optimize, and scale the deployment. Enterprise plans include FDE support.
