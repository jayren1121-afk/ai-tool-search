What happens if I add seats mid-year on an annual plan?
---
You will continue to get monthly invoices for newly added seats unless you proactively add those seats to your annual subscription or remove the seats.
---
What are AI credits and how do they work?
---
3,000 credits/month on the Professional plan
3,500 credits/month on the Organization plan
4,250 credits/month on the Enterprise plan
---
All other seats (Collab, Dev, and View) and users on the Starter plan receive 500 credits/month.
---
If you need more credits, you can purchase additional AI credits on your plan. For more details, check out our Help Center article on how AI credits work.
