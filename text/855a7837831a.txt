PASSIONATE ABOUT MUSIC
Our Commitment to Artists & 100 % Royalty Free AI Music

SOUNDRAW is an AI music generator built by musicians for the purpose of making music creation easy and accessible to everyone. Every beat, melody, and stem you create here is powered by proprietary algorithms trained exclusively on music produced in-house by our professional team.
That means:


No scraped songs, no legal gray areas – we own the master recordings and publishing rights to every note that trains our AI.
Instant, copyright-free beats for YouTube, podcasts, games, and commercial releases. Keep 100 % of the royalties, forever.
Download full stems or mix directly in our browser-based mixer to fine-tune tempo, key, and instrument layers.
Because we control the entire catalog, you can create with confidence—no takedown notices, no hidden licenses, just limitless creativity.

We are proud to be a supporter of aiformusic, alongside Roland and UMG. Aiformusic is an initiative with principles centered around the responsible use of AI in music.
