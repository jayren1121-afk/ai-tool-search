Frequently Asked Questions
What forms of payment do you accept?
Rytr supports payments from all major credit and debit cards. We accept all currencies and prices are given in USD with an exchange rate applied at the time of purchase determined by our payment provider, Stripe.
What’s the cancellation policy?
With our monthly subscription you can cancel any time and will continue to have access to your remaining credits for that month. Our annual plan enables you to save 20% off the total price and comes with a 12 month commitment. You can read our full policy here.
Can I upgrade later?
Absolutely! Head to the credits section of your account and you can upgrade to the Unlimited or Premium plan whenever you like.
Who owns the content created?
The content created by Rytr is owned by the user or the entity that utilizes Rytr to generate the content. However, all generated content must adhere to Rytr’s Terms of Service. While users maintain ownership of their content, it’s important to remember that usage of Rytr must comply with these terms and guidelines.
