Pricing FAQ
How do coworkers get assigned to my work?
When you hire a tier, the coworkers in that tier are immediately available to your team. Your Chief of Staff coordinates which coworkers handle which tasks based on the workflow you define.
What's included in implementation?
Implementation is included at every tier. Options scale from a self-service experience for solo users and small teams to a fully managed rollout led by a Forward Deployed Engineer for enterprise customers. Your account manager will walk you through the options that fit your team.
Can I add workspaces?
Yes, from Anticipate up. Anticipate includes 2 workspaces and Autonomous includes 7, and you can add more at your plan's workspace add-on price. Adapt and Accelerate run in a single workspace, so if you need a second one you move up a tier.
What if I outgrow my tier?
You can upgrade anytime. Your existing setup, Smartwords balance, and skills come with you.
What happens if I run out of Smartwords?
Smartwords are an annual allowance. If you need more before renewal, you can top up at any point in your contract year without changing plan. On Anticipate and Autonomous your balance is shared across workspaces, so unused words aren't stranded in one team.
Do I pay twice for content I've already translated?
No. Translation memory matches and repetitions are free on every plan, including Adapt. Once a sentence has been approved, reusing it anywhere in your content costs no Smartwords — so your effective cost per word falls as your translation memory grows.
Do you offer a free trial?
Yes. 15-day free trial, including API access.
How does the Marketplace work?
Marketplace gives you on-demand access to 500K+ vetted human experts: linguists, copywriters, reviewers.
What languages do you support?
280+ languages across translation and content creation.
What security and compliance standards does Smartcat meet?
Smartcat is SOC 2 Type II compliant. Your content is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is controlled by user roles, and actions are logged for audits. Your content is never used to train public AI models. A Data Processing Agreement is available, and on written request Smartcat deletes or returns your data within 30 days. From the Anticipate plan up, you also get SSO, 2FA and a completed technical questionnaire for your security review.
Why use Smartcat instead of ChatGPT or Claude for translation?
Smartcat is built for content you publish, not one-off chats. Every job uses your glossary, style guide and translation memory, so terms stay consistent across 280+ languages. Smartcat handles 80+ file formats, sends work through review and approval steps, connects to 40+ tools your team already uses, and brings in vetted human experts when you need them. In 2026, 92% of words translated by Smartcat AI for paying customers were accepted without a single human edit.
