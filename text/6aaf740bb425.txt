MODELS
The best for the job

Access Fable 5, Claude Opus 5, GPT-5.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro, GLM 5.2, Kimi K3, and more. Pick the best model for the task. Run different tasks with different models in parallel, or switch between models for a given task, all without leaving your workflow.

INTEGRATIONS
Agents where you work

Connect Warp to Slack, Linear, and GitHub to get AI help right inside your tools. Mention @Warp to investigate bugs, suggest fixes, or open PRs, all without context-switching. With real-time session links and shared visibility, anyone can see, guide, or review what the agent is doing.

YOUR PRIVACY AND SECURITY
Transparency and control at every touchpoint.
Your data, secure and private

Warp is SOC 2 compliant and has Zero Data Retention policies with all contracted LLM providers: no customer data is retained, stored, or used for training. App telemetry can be individually configured and automatically enforced for teams.

Complete control of your agents

Control exactly how much autonomy agents have, from approving every step to allowing full autonomy. Configurable at the individual and enterprise level.

Full transparency

You can read the list of high-level telemetry events sent for app analytics and monitor telemetry in real time with our native Network Log.
