FAQ
Questions about Final Round AI

Answers about AI interview assistance, pricing, privacy and supported interview formats.

Is there an AI assistant for interviews?

Yes. Final Round AI is an AI interview assistant for job seekers. Interview CoPilot™ can listen during supported live or practice interview scenarios, understand the question, and provide private, role-specific answer guidance based on your resume, job description and prep materials.

Can AI help me in an interview?

AI can help you prepare stronger examples, practice out loud, structure answers and stay focused during an interview. Final Round AI combines preparation, real-time guidance, practice interviews and post-interview debriefs so you can improve across the full interview loop.

How much do AI interview assistants cost?

AI interview assistant pricing varies by product and plan. Final Round AI offers a free plan for exploring the app, while live Interview CoPilot requires a paid Pro subscription. Visit the pricing page for the current plan details.

What interview types does Final Round AI support?

Final Round AI supports, but is not limited to, general, behavioral, coding, system design, phone, HireVue-style and role-specific interviews. Interview CoPilot adapts to the question and context instead of requiring you to manage separate products for each format.

Does Final Round AI work for practice and live interviews?

Yes. You can use Final Round AI before an interview to create a Goal, attach your resume and materials, and run practice interviews. During a supported live interview, Interview CoPilot provides private real-time guidance, then writes a debrief after the session.

Is Final Round AI visible during screen sharing?

Interview CoPilot includes Stealth Mode, which is designed to keep Final Round AI's private guidance windows out of supported screen shares and recordings. It is on by default in the desktop app, and you can manage it in Settings under Privacy & Stealth.
