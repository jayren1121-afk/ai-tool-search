FAQ

Choose your plan according to your monetization and scale

Which plan should I choose?

Creator is for personal and non-monetized content. Pro adds commercial usage, monetization and the full Track Editor. Business is designed for client work, agencies, apps, games and broader commercial use.

What's the difference between Creator and Pro?

Both plans include higher generation limits, lossless downloads, Quick Remix and custom track duration. Pro additionally includes commercial usage, monetization and the full Track Editor with structure, stem, BPM and key controls.

What's included in Quick Remix?

Quick Remix lets you regenerate or remove stems from an existing track while keeping the rest of the track. Quick Remix is available on all plans. Changing track duration is available on paid plans.

How does the Track Editor work?

The Track Editor lets Pro and Business users prepare multiple changes to an existing track, including its structure, stems, duration, BPM and key. Changes are not generated in real time.

You first select everything you want to change and then explicitly generate a new version of the track. One generated result uses one generation from your monthly limit. Your original track remains unchanged.

Can I use Mubert music commercially?

Creator is intended for non-commercial content. Commercial usage and monetization require Pro or Business depending on the use case.

Can I publish generated tracks to Spotify or other music streaming services?

No. Mubert tracks cannot be redistributed as standalone music or registered in Content ID systems. They can be licensed as music used within eligible content and projects according to the selected plan.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

You cannot use previously generated tracks in new projects after the subscription ends. Projects published while the relevant subscription was active can remain published and continue to be monetized according to their existing license.

What if I receive a copyright claim?

Mubert music is royalty-free, but false-positive claims may occasionally happen. The license certificate supplied with the downloaded track can be used to confirm your right to use it.

Are payments refundable?

Payments are generally non-refundable because access to digital content is provided immediately. If a user selected the wrong plan and wants to upgrade, they can contact Support.
