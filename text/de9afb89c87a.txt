HOW USAGE WORKS

What’s a credit?

Credits measure the work Lindy does. You only use them when Lindy is working.

Everyday Asks
2-250 Credits

Answers, summaries, lookups and updates in your tools, closing out a meeting, drafting a reply.

Deep work
250-1,000 Credits

Research a competitor and write the report, triage a day's support queue, turn a week of calls into a proposal, build basic html artifacts.

Big Builds
1,000-2,500 Credits

Complex html dashboards, multi-media decks built from your CRM and analytics, internal tools shipped end to end, heavy scheduled workflows.

Model agnostic

Pick your model of choice for each task. Most major models are available.

One pool, whole team

Every seat adds its credits to a shared pool, so anyone can draw on it when they need more.

No surprise bills

If the pool runs low, Lindy pauses and tells you. Admins can set credit allocations per seat.
