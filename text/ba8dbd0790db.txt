Frequently asked questions
Can I use Whimsical for free?
Yes! With our Free plan, you can have unlimited boards, unlimited workspace members, unlimited guests, 10 AI credits per member, and more. Additional AI credits can be purchased separately on any plan.
What types of payment do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards. For Business plan customers with more than 10 editor seats, we also offer invoicing, including wire and ACH transfers. Contact sales to learn more.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits?
Yes, we gladly offer a 20% discount to non-profit organizations. Contact us to learn more.
How many free workspaces can I create?
You can create 2 free workspaces and switch between them seamlessly.
Do you offer discounts for students and teachers?
Yes, students and teachers can use Whimsical free of charge. Sign up and fill out this form to apply.
Can I collaborate with people outside my organization?
Yes, you can share a file with anyone using a share link, or invite people into your workspace as guests. Guests are free and unlimited on every plan, including Free.
