Price should be determined by value created
It's easy to justify paying for things you love. When you get a lot of value out of a service, it feels fair to pay a high price for it.
---
Since Sunsama is (hopefully) something that you love using every single day you're at work to feel calm and effective, we chose a price of ~$1/workday for the daily value of the service.

What are my payment options?
You can pay for your subscription using any major credit card.
---
What happens if I invite colleagues to join my workspace?
Sunsama costs $25/month per person ($20/month for yearly plans). So if you invited one teammate to your workspace, the total monthly cost would be $50.
---
Can I cancel? How does that work? Refunds?
You can cancel your subscription at any point. When you cancel, you can continue using Sunsama until the end of the billing cycle.
