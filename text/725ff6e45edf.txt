Helpful Answers
Is there a student/teacher/non-profit discount?

Yes there is! We offer a 20% discount for students, teachers, and non-profit organizations. After you submit payment, please let us know and we'll refund the 20% from the initial payment, and all future payments will be charged at the discounted rate.

Can I still access Maestra if I cancel?

Yes you can. You'll still have access to all your transcripts, captions, and voiceovers, and can still use features such as public links and embeddable players.

Can I work on the same file with other people?

Yes, you can. you can share individual files by clicking the "Share" button on the file. If you're looking to collaborate on files, you can use "Maestra Teams" to collaborate with your teams and colleagues in real-time.

Is my payment secure?

Yes it is. Your payment is processed using Stripe, world's one of the largest payment processors. Not even we can see your credit card information!

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit/debit cards from: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, JCB, Diners Club, and China UnionPay.

Can I access my account on different computers?

Yes you can. Maestra is a cloud based online software which means you can log in and edit your files on almost any laptop or desktop with an internet connection.

Are there any limitations on generating voiceovers?

You can generate up to 3000 characters per minute (or per credit), and the same additional amount for each translation. (Average speech contains 750 characters per minute.)

What if I have more questions?

We're always happy to hear from you! You can send us an email at support@maestra.ai for any questions, comments, or concerns.
