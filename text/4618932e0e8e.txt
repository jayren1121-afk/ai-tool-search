The “total treasury return” posted (between 3.56% and 3.91%) is not the DGVXX 7-day yield, it is an annualized compounded number based on the current 7-day yield for the BNY Dreyfus Government Cash Management Fund (DGVXX) (3.50%) and the additional return paid by Brex Treasury LLC, and assumes (1) that yield will remain static, and (2) all dividends and distributions will be reinvested and held for one year, compounding at that static rate.

The additional-return rate is a Brex-set nominal rate based on your applicable balance tier and is an annualized, compounded figure. This means that if additional returns are not held for a year, the realized additional return will be lower than the displayed rate. Your tier is determined daily based on your total Brex balance across Checking, Treasury, and Vault. It may change as that balance changes; Brex may also change the tier schedule at any time. Actual payouts are calculated daily and may be lower or higher.

The total return shown is an annualized estimate. It assumes the current DGVXX yield and your applicable additional return rate remain unchanged for one year, and that monthly payouts are reinvested. Actual returns will vary because fund yields, your balance tier, and your Treasury balance may change. Additional return is calculated daily and paid monthly on funds invested in Treasury. More information can be found here.

DGVXX yields fluctuate daily, and past yields are not indicative of future yields. This means that if dividends and additional returns are not held for a year, or if rates fall, the actual realized total treasury return will be lower than the rate displayed.

The posted rate is based on customers who are eligible for the highest-tier rate on their Brex business account. Yield and additional return are variable and are earned only on invested funds in Treasury. They are calculated daily, provided monthly, and automatically reinvested.
