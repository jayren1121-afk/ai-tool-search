FAQs
What are the benefits of a subscription and which plan should I choose?
Subscribing unlocks advanced editing tools, an exclusive content library, increased cloud storage, and an ad-free experience on the mobile app. Choose Pro for standard photo and video editing, or Ultra if you require heavy AI generation, premium AI models, and team collaboration features.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?
You can upgrade from Pro to Ultra at any time if purchased directly through Picsart.com. If you downgrade a plan, the new billing cycle will start after your current one expires. More information can be found in this article.
How can I pay for a subscription, and are there discounts available?
We accept credit/debit cards, PayPal, and Apple/Google Pay, and we do not offer installment plans. You can save up to 60% by choosing an annual subscription over a monthly one, and you can find additional promos by checking the mobile app or the website frequently.
How do I cancel my subscription or request a refund?
You can cancel your subscription by following the instructions here. Refunds for mobile app subscriptions are subject to Apple or Google Play Store policies, while website purchases can be requested via this link. If you're not sure where you originally purchased your subscription, you can refer to this article to help identify your payment source.
What are credits and how can I get more?
Credits are virtual tokens used for generative AI tools. If you need more, you can upgrade to the Ultra plan or purchase "add-on" credits through your profile settings on the mobile app or website.
Do unused credits roll over?
Monthly subscription credits expire at the end of each billing cycle and do not roll over. However, any purchased add-on credits remain in your account and will not expire until used.
Will I get my credits back if a generation fails?
Yes, if a generation is unsuccessful, your consumed credits will be automatically rolled back to your account within 24-48 hours.
Can I use my benefits across multiple devices and platforms?
Yes, you can access your Picsart subscription benefits seamlessly across iOS, Android, web, and the Microsoft Store by logging into the same Picsart account used for your purchase. To link a new device, simply log in with your Picsart account on that device and it will be added automatically. To remove it, you do it through the "Manage Devices" section in your app settings.
Will I still have access to my benefits if I cancel?
Yes, you will continue to have full access to all subscription benefits until the current billing period expires.
