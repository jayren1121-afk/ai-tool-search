What are tokens?

Tokens are how we measure usage of AI features in Chronicle.

Here are how many approximate tokens are needed for different actions in Chronicle:

Remix = ~1 token

Generate presentation = ~10 tokens + 1 token per chapter

Rewrite content = ~1 token

 

Check out Settings → Billing for the most up to date token count per plan.

Are tokens shared within a workspace?

No, tokens are unique to each user in the workspace.

How can I get more tokens?

The easiest way to get more tokens is by upgrading to a Pro or Plus plan.

Soon, you will be able to purchase additional tokens as add-ons.

Do unused tokens expire?

Unused tokens expire at the end of each month.

Can I change my plan or cancel at any time?

You can cancel or downgrade at any time.

If you downgrade from a paid plan, your content remains yours and you will maintain access to all your created content, but some features may be limited. For instance, when you downgrade from a Plus plan, your collaborators will lose access to the workspace.

Is there a discount on annual billing?

Yes! You can save up to 20% by choosing an annual plan compared to monthly billing.

How do I get charged when adding a member to my workspace?

You can add as many members to your workspace as you’d like on the Plus plan. You will be charged for an additional seat for each member who accepts their invitation.

What AI models does Chronicle use?

We leverage a variety of world-class AI models to provide you with the best possible outputs for content and image creation.

We currently work with models from Anthropic and OpenAI, alongside our own custom-built tools.

We continually evaluate and update our model stack to ensure you’re getting the highest-quality outputs in Chronicle. The models we use may evolve as new models emerge.
