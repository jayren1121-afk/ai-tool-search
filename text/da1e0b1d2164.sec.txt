Frequently Asked Questions
Is there a free trial available?
Yes. Every new account gets 5,000 free words and 25 credits, no credit card required. Just register and start creating. You can upgrade to a paid plan anytime.
How long do the credits last?
An annual plan gives you the whole year's words and credits up front, and they last 12 months. On a monthly plan, unused words and credits move to your rollover bank at each renewal.
---

What is the rollover bank?
On monthly plans, the words and credits you don't use in a month are saved in your rollover bank, up to 3 months of your plan's allowance. Banked words and credits don't expire. They are spent after your monthly allowance runs out. The bank
Can I add more brands?
Yes. Extra brand slots are $29/month each, up to 10, on any paid plan. You can change the number of slots from your account page.
Can I buy another annual subscription if I already have one?
Yes. The credits from your existing annual plan stay separate and keep their original expiration date, and the new plan's credits last a full year. Switching away from an annual plan never removes its credits.
