FAQ

How does pricing work?

Plans are sized to your lead volume, mailboxes, and dialer seats. Request pricing and we'll build the right plan with you.

What does implementation look like?

Every plan starts with onboarding sessions and CSM support in a shared Slack channel. Scale adds a dedicated CSM, and Enterprise adds a forward-deployed strategist who builds and launches your rollout with you.

What happens in the demo?

A 30-minute walkthrough of how Ava would run your outbound, tailored to your ICP and current stack. We'll scope the right plan together.

Can we control what Ava sends?

Yes. Approve before sending, lock tone and CTAs, set banned phrases, and coach Ava's writing. Autonomous by default, adjustable by design.

Who does Ava send emails as?

Ava sends in your reps' names, from your own sending domains, so outreach reads like it came from them. Ava handles the replies, your reps get the meetings.

Is Artisan secure and compliant?

Artisan is SOC 2 Type II certified and supports SSO / SAML, advanced security controls, and audit logs.

Which tools does Ava integrate with?

Salesforce and HubSpot sync bi-directionally, custom webhooks bring in your own data, and Slack keeps your team on top of performance.

Does Ava replace our BDR team?

Ava runs the repetitive outbound work, finding leads, writing outreach, handling replies, so your reps spend their time on live conversations. You set the guardrails: approvals, escalation rules, and when a human steps in.
