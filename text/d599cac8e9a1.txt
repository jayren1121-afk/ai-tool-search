Frequently asked questions.
What’s your pricing?

It depends on the Premium plan you chose. Scroll to the top of this page for a comprehensive overview of our Premium plans. It’s quicker than having to read a written explanation.

But remember, most people need anywhere from three to six months to find a new job. What’s more, you should tailor your resume to each job opening you apply to, as it increases your chance of getting hired. Because of that, we recommend that you choose the yearly plan.

Can I download it for free?

Yes, you can as long as you stick to free customization options. In the end, you will be able to create a nice resume for free. You only pay for the more advanced customization options (they are clearly labeled inside the app).

Do you have a one-time download?

You can download your resume as many times as you want. We don’t limit the number of downloads. But remember, you will need to upgrade to a paid subscription plan if you want to use more advanced customization options.

Can I get a free trial?

Kickresume is free as long as you don’t select paid customization options.

I’m a student. Can I have a free subscription?

Yes. Kickresume is free for all students who can prove their student status with ISIC, ITIC or UNiDAYS.
