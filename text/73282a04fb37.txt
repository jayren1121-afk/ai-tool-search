FAQS

Any questions?

Is Pitch free to use?

Yes, Pitch’s Free plan lets you create unlimited presentations forever.

You can learn more about our Plus, Team, and Business plans on our pricing page. Each plan’s price depends on the amount of seats you need and is billed on a monthly or annual subscription.

Can I hire Pitch to build my presentation for me?

We don’t offer custom design services, but you’re in good hands. Use Pitch Agent to generate a presentation in seconds, or choose from over 150 templates in our gallery for a head start. We can also recommend great creative agencies familiar with Pitch if you’d like further help.

Does Pitch offer a free trial?

Free trials are available to some customers depending on their needs. Please contact support if you would like more information about this option.

Can I import my existing deck, and if so, in what format?

Yes! You can upload a PPTX file. If you’re coming from Google Slides, Keynote, or another tool, export to PPTX so you can use our importer.
