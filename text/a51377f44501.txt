Credits
What happens if I run out of credits?

If you run out of credits, you'll still be able to use free features like creating Splicer images, or generating slow Collager images. If you run out of credits and want more, you can renew your plan early or upgrade. Both options give you more credits.

How do I buy more credits?

When you run out of credits, we'll show you a link so you can upgrade or renew your plan early. Both options give you more credits. You can also see your credit balance as well as buy more credits at any time from the account page.

Do credits roll over?

We're working on adding this, but for now credits reset at the beginning of your billing period. So if you're on a monthly plan, that's every month. On a yearly plan, that's every year. If you'd like updates on credit roll-over and other Artbreeder features, come say hi in our Discord server.
