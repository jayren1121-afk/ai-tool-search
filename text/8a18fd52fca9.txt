We believe in fair Pricing

Authors already have enough to worry about. We don't want to add to that. That's why we offer a simple, fair pricing model that scales with your needs.

21-day Free Trial

Get unrestricted access to all of the features of Novelcrafter for 21 days. No credit card required! See for yourself if you like it before committing.

We don't do Sales

No special sales, discounts or holiday promotions. That means no fake countdown timers and no artificially inflated prices - you'll get the best price, every day of the year.

Pay for what you need

We tailored each plan so you only need pay for the features you need, making sure you get the most value for your money. You can always upgrade or downgrade later.

Frequently asked questions
How does the free trial work?

When you sign up, you will immediately have access to all the features of Novelcrafter for 21 days. If you decide to continue with your plan, you will be charged the monthly or yearly price, depending on your choice, at the end of the trial period.

Is there a free plan?

No, we currently do not offer a free plan. However, we do offer a 21-day free trial, so you can try out Novelcrafter before committing to a plan.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, including Visa, Mastercard, and American Express, as well as PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. For Chinese users, we also accept Alipay.

Do you offer discounts for students, educators or non-profits?

No. We currently do not offer discounts on our plans.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

If you no longer have an active subscription, you will still have read-only access to your data. This means that you can view and export your data, but you will not be able to add or edit.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. If you upgrade, you will be charged the prorated amount for the rest of the month.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. If you cancel, you will be able to use your plan until the end of your billing cycle, and you will not be charged again.

Can I change my billing frequency?

Yes, you can change your billing frequency at any time. If you change your billing frequency, you will be charged the prorated amount for the rest of the month.

Can I pause my subscription?

Yes, you can pause your subscription at any time. If you pause your subscription, you will not be charged again until you resume your subscription. This means the subscription will run until the end of the billing cycle, and then it will be paused. You can resume your subscription afterwards.

Which plan do my collaborators need?

Your collaborators will need to have any paid plan if they want to add or edit data. If they only need to view data, a free account is sufficient.

Do you offer student discounts?

No, at this time we do not offer student or educational discounts. However, please let us know if you're interested in this, as we are gauging interest for future offerings.

Do you have Black Friday or holiday sales?

No, we do not offer any special sales or discounts during Black Friday or holiday seasons. Our pricing remains consistent throughout the year, so you never have to worry about missing out on a deal or paying more than necessary.

Do you offer team-based pricing or plans?

No, at this time we do not offer unified team-based pricing or subscriptions. For the time being, each user will need to have their own account and subscription.

Can I pay in a currency other than my country's currency?

Yes, if you want to pay in a different currency, please contact support to request a customized payment link.

Do you offer Purchase Power Parity (PPP)?

No, we currently do not offer localized pricing adjustments. However, we are in the process of evaluating this option with our payment provider and may implement it in the future.
