How Wordly Pricing Works
Packages are for a 12-month term, so you have 12 months to use the hours you purchase, and you can use them across multiple sessions throughout the year.
You can add more hours at any time during your term. Unused hours at the end of the year can roll over - ask your sales rep for more information.
You only pay for the time you use. For example, if you have a 30 minute session, you only pay for 30 mintues. We don't round up.
You get access to 4 products for 1 fixed price - translation, captions, transcripts, & summaries.
You get access to all languages for 1 fixed price.

Wordly Discounts

Wordly is powered by AI technology which enables us to offer translation for significantly less than human interpreters. We offer several discount options:

Volume Discounts - Save from 10% to 30% or more by purchasing larger packages. The more you buy, the more you save.
Multi-year Discounts - Save more by committing to a 2 or 3 year agreement.
Non-profit and NGO Discounts - Save an additional 10% off the price of Wordly Pro (25 hours) or higher all language packages.
Educational Discounts - Save an additional 10% off the price of Wordly Pro (25 hours) or higher all language packages.
Pricing FAQs
Purchase Quantities (Hours) - Can I purchase in quantities other than 10, 50, and 200 hours? Yes, you can purchase Wordly hours in any quantity you want. The pricing table shows the most common purchase quantities - those quantities are tied to increasing discount levels. Contact sales to find out about volume discounts.

Online Purchases - Can I purchase online? Yes. You can purchase online at the Wordly Store. You need to create a Wordly Account. If you are a new customer we recommend you speak with a Wordly representative to ensure you purchase the right package.
Account Activation - How long does it take to activate a new account? New accounts are usually activated within 5-10 minutes of your purchase. We recommend new accounts complete an onboarding session with a Customer Success Rep to make sure you can have a great event.
Payment Terms - When do I need to pay for my purchase? If you purchase online, payment is collected at that time. If you purchase with our Sales team, payment is due upon receipt of our invoice. You need to complete payment prior to your first event.
