What is a credit?
A credit is Dust's unit for measuring AI usage. Credit consumption depends on the model used, the complexity of the task, and any tools the agent uses, such as search, data retrieval, code execution, or actions in connected apps. Learn more

Do unused credits roll over?
No. Each seat's monthly credit allocation resets at the start of every billing period. This keeps pricing predictable and makes it easier for teams to plan.

What's the difference between Free, Pro, and Max seats?
---
These are seat types within the Business plan. Admins assign one to each user based on that individual's expected usage:
---
Free: $0, 500 credits lifetime. Best for occasional users or people trying Dust.
Pro: $30/month, or $24/month billed yearly, with 8,000 credits/month. Best for most team members.
Max: $150/month, or $120/month billed yearly, with 40,000 credits/month. Best for power users running complex automations, Deep research, or tool-heavy workflows regularly.
---
You can mix and match seat types across your workspace and reassign them anytime as usage changes. Learn more about seats.
