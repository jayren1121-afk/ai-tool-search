FAQ

Questions, answered
What is Decktopus AI?

Decktopus AI is an end-to-end presentation platform powered by AI. Enter a topic or upload a file, and it generates a complete, ready-to-present deck with structure, content, and design included.

What's the difference between Pro and Business?

 Pro is for one person: 750 AI credits/month, full Loop access, all export options. Business adds team features — shared workspace, Brand Kit, slide analytics, custom domain, priority support — plus 1,000 AI credits per seat/month.

Can I cancel anytime?

 Yes. Cancel anytime from your account settings — no cancellation fees. You'll keep full access until the end of your current billing period, then your plan won't renew.

Do you offer a student discount?

Yes. Students get 50% off all plans with the code SUPPORTEDUCATION.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Your data is excluded from AI training by default.
