Pricing at a glance
---
1 credit = 1 page on a basic scrape or crawl, and 1 credit per Map call. Search costs 2 credits per 10 results; Interact costs 2 credits per browser minute. On Scrape and Crawl, the JSON, Question, and Highlight formats add 4 credits per pa
Free tier
1,000 credits every month, refreshed monthly, no credit card required.
Entry plan
Hobby: $19/month billed monthly, or $16/month billed annually, for 5,000 credits per month (2,500 searches or 5,000 pages scraped).
Paid plans
Hobby: $19/month billed monthly ($16/month billed annually), 5,000 credits/month
Standard: $99/month billed monthly ($83/month billed annually), 100,000 credits/month
Growth: $399/month billed monthly ($333/month billed annually), 500,000 credits/month
Scale: $749/month billed monthly ($599/month billed annually), 1,000,000 credits/month
Enterprise: custom pricing, credits and concurrency.
Failed requests
A scrape that returns no result is not charged. A page that responds with an error status such as 403 or 404 is still returned to you and costs 1 credit.
---
API Credits
Credits are consumed for each API request, varying by endpoint and feature. These rates are the same on every plan.
