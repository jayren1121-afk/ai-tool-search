---

What is an LSU?
---
LangChain Standard Units ("LSUs") are the standard unit of measure for usage consumption across all LangSmith products and features. The number of LSUs consumed depends on each product's metering rate and usage. 1 LSU = $1.

LangSmith Deployment Questions
Does LangSmith Deployment include any free deployments?
---
Developer: 7 LSUs included per organization each month. After the allowance is used, Fleet pauses new runs until the allowance resets or the organization upgrades to Plus.
Plus: 37 LSUs included per organization each month. Additional usage is billed.

LangSmith Engine Questions
How is LangSmith Engine priced?
---
Engine is metered in LSUs (LangChain Standard Units), which are consumed during each Engine run. 1 LSU costs $1.
---
What does a LangChain Standard Unit (LSU) represent?
---
Plus plans: LSUs are an additional charge on top of your monthly license fee and any other product usage.
---
Enterprise plans: LSUs are charged against your committed LangChain credits.
