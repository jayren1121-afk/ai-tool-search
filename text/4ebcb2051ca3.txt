How do I choose between Starter, Lite, and Pro?

Starter is best for testing the service and checking output quality before committing to a paid plan. Lite works well for regular everyday use. Pro is the better choice if you need more Fast Queue minutes, access to the VST plugin, local processing in the desktop app, or API access.

Which plans include the VST plugin?

The VST plugin is available on the Pro plan only.
