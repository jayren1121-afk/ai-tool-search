Pricing FAQs
What does “Unlimited AI video generation” mean?

Unlimited AI video generation means there is no limit on the number of videos you can create. However, video length, AI Dubbing usage, and generative credit consumption are subject to the limits of your selected plan. In other words, while the number of videos you can generate is unlimited, resource usage is managed based on your plan.

What’s the difference between the Free, Personal, and Team plans?

The Free plan is designed for trying out AI video creation and basic AI Dubbing. It allows a limited number of videos per month, with restrictions on video length and access to generative models. It’s suitable for exploring the basic creation workflow.

The Personal plan is intended for individual creators. It allows unlimited AI video generation with videos up to 30 minutes long and includes advanced AI Dubbing features with lip-sync. You can use the included generative credits and AI Dubbing minutes for regular content production, and purchase additional generative credits if needed.

The Team plan is built for collaborative team environments. Generative credits are provided per seat and shared across the team, enabling flexible usage. It also includes increased AI Dubbing minutes and Proofread features to review and edit translated scripts, making it suitable for team-based content operations and review workflows.

What’s the difference between monthly and yearly billing?

The main difference between monthly and yearly billing is the payment schedule and pricing. Yearly billing is offered at a discounted rate compared to monthly billing and is charged once per year.

Generative credits are granted based on the billing cycle for both monthly and yearly plans. For yearly billing, the total annual generative credits are granted upfront.

AI Dubbing minutes are provided on a monthly basis regardless of billing type. Even with a yearly subscription, AI Dubbing minutes reset every month and do not roll over.

What are generative credits?

Generative credits are usage units consumed when creating generative images or videos using external generative video and image model APIs. Credits are also used when selecting generative media in automated features such as Topic to Video. In addition, if you exceed the AI Dubbing minutes included in your plan, additional AI Dubbing usage is deducted from your generative credits. The number of credits consumed depends on the selected feature and generative model.

How is AI Dubbing usage calculated?

AI Dubbing usage is calculated based on the total number of minutes provided per month. The included AI Dubbing minutes can be used freely across multiple videos, and only the actual dubbing duration is deducted. Once the included minutes are fully used, you can continue using AI Dubbing, with additional usage deducted from your generative credits. AI Dubbing minutes reset at the start of each billing cycle and do not roll over.

Can I purchase additional credits if I run out?

Some features, such as custom avatars and generative credits, can be purchased as Add-ons on paid plans.

Add-ons follow the same billing cycle as your base subscription. If you are on a monthly plan, Add-ons are billed monthly. If you are on a yearly plan, Add-ons are billed annually. Add-ons are managed together with your subscription, and any plan changes or cancellations apply to Add-ons under the same terms.

Do unused credits or AI Dubbing minutes roll over?

Generative credits and AI Dubbing minutes included in your plan reset each billing cycle and do not roll over. For monthly plans, usage resets every month. For yearly plans, included usage is still subject to the billing cycle rules. AI Dubbing minutes are always provided and reset on a monthly basis, even for yearly subscriptions. Enterprise plans may follow different rules depending on contract terms.

How do plan changes and cancellations work?

You can change your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, allowing you to access the new plan’s features and limits right away. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. Even after canceling, you will retain access to your plan until the billing period ends.

What’s included in the Enterprise plan?

The Enterprise plan provides customized AI video solutions for large teams and organizations.

Depending on your contract, generative credits and AI Dubbing minutes can be flexibly configured, and additional generative models can be enabled. Enterprise plans also support bulk video generation, Proofread-based review workflows, enterprise-grade security (such as SAML SSO), and dedicated technical support.
