FAQs
What are Credits?

Credits are the resources you use when generating videos and images. Different actions you perform on LTX Studio - such as rendering videos, changing character appearance, or creating new shots - consume different amounts of credits. We measure your usage with credits to ensure you make the most out of your plan and are billed for the actual resources you use.

How do I track my credit usage?

Your current credit balance is shown at the top of the app and updates in real time after each action.

Can I switch subscription plans?

Yes! You can switch between subscription plans at any time to suit your changing creative needs. Simply go to the 'Pricing' tab in your Account Settings, where you can view all available plans. Click 'Subscribe' on your new preferred plan or use the 'Change Plan' button in the 'Manage account' tab.

Upgrading your plan: When you upgrade, the change will take effect immediately, and you will be offered a prorated price based on the remaining time in your current cycle.

Downgrading your plan: Downgrades will only take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription easily from the "Manage account" tab in your Account Settings. There, you will find a "Cancel Plan" button within the "Your Plan" section. When you choose to cancel, your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period. This way, you can continue creating until the last day of your subscription. For more information, please visit our refund policy.

What is your refund policy?

You can request a refund by contacting us at here. Refunds are typically granted for requests made within 14 days of payment, provided that usage does not exceed 1200 credits. We are committed to user satisfaction and strive to ensure that if our platform doesn't meet your expectations early on, you can get your money back. For more information, please visit our refund policy.

What is included under 'Personal Use'?

"Personal Use" refers to using LTX Studio for creating content that you can post on your personal social media profiles and personal websites. It does not include any promotional activities for businesses, services, or products, nor does it cover content that is sponsored or created in partnership with third parties. Your usage should be strictly non-commercial and not affiliated with any business-oriented objectives. If you're unsure whether your intended use qualifies, feel free to contact our support team for clarification.

What is included under 'Commercial Use'?

"Commercial Use" for LTX Studio subscribers allows businesses, marketers, and professional creators to leverage the platform’s capabilities for commercial purposes. This includes the creation and distribution of content that promotes a business, product, or service across various media channels. Commercial use also covers sponsored content, content created in partnership with third parties, and any use where you are paid or compensated for the content produced using LTX Studio.

What is included under 'Enhanced data privacy'?

The user's input and output won’t be used to train our model.

Why do I now see "credits" instead of "computing seconds" in the platform?

We renamed computing seconds to credits to better reflect how generation works and make the experience more intuitive. It's the same balance—just a clearer name.
