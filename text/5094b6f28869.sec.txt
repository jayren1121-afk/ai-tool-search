New users receive a one-time balance of 100 free credits. These do not refresh daily or monthly.

Do my monthly credits roll over?
No, credits reset at the start of each billing cycle.
Can I buy more credits?
Yes, you can buy more credits under your profile.
I have credits. Why does the app still ask me to upgrade?
Some tools are only available on paid plans even if you still have credits. Credits cover usage, but feature access depends on your plan tier. If the tool is plan-restricted, you'll still see the upgrade screen.
