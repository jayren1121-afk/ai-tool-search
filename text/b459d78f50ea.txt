A flat pricing per workspace

At Crisp, we offer a straightforward and easy-to-understand pricing model with a flat rate per workspace. This means you pay a single, consistent price regardless of the number of conversations you have each month. Our approach ensures that you can scale your customer support operations without overspending. With Crisp, you get all the essential tools and AI-powered features your team needs to provide exceptional customer service, all included in one simple, predictable subscription. Simplify your budgeting and focus on what matters most – growing your business and delighting your customers.
