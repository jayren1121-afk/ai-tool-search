FAQs
Polycam overview
What are image limits?

Images limits indicate the maximum number of photos you can upload to create a 3D model using Object mode:


Free plan: Up to 150 images per model
Basic plan: Up to 300 images per model
Business and Enterprise plans: Up to 2,000 images per model
What devices can I use Polycam on?

Polycam is available on multiple platforms:


iOS devices (iPhone, iPad, Apple Vision Pro)
Android devices
Web browsers

Important Note: Space Mode scanning is exclusively available on compatible iOS device.

‍Learn more here

What happens if I reach my capture limit on a plan?

You have two options when you reach your plan's capture limit:


Delete existing captures to free up space
Upgrade your plan for a higher capture limit
Do higher-priced subscription plans provide better-quality captures?

The core capture technology remains consistent across all subscription plans. However, paid plans offer advantages that can lead to better final results:


Higher image limits (up to 2,000 images vs 150 on free plan)
More images generally result in more detailed and accurate 3D models
Particularly beneficial for larger objects or complex scenes

So while the base capture quality is the same, paid plans enable you to create potentially higher-quality models through increased image allowances.

What file types can Polycam export?

All Plans:


.gltf

Basic Plan:


3D Models: .obj, .fbx, .dae, .usdz, .stl, .ply, .las, .pts, .xyz
Documentation: .pdf, .png, .svg, .csv
Media: Blueprints, images, videos
CAD: .dxf

Business & Enterprise Plans:


All Basic plan formats
Geo-referenced .las files
Access to TransformEngine

Please note, export formats are dependent on capture mode used.

‍Learn more here

How can I be sure my data is secure?

Polycam prioritizes data security by storing your captures securely with industry-standard encryption. Your captures will not be shared unless you publish or share them directly.

Plans and payments
Do you still offer the Pro plan?
We have streamlined our set of plans to better focus on our individual and team users, and are no longer offering the Pro plan. Anyone subscribed to Pro prior to this change is now a Legacy Pro user and keeps the Pro price and Pro features as long as they stay subscribed.
Which plan is right for me?

Basic Plan Built for creators and freelancers producing professional-quality 3D models and gaussian splats. Get access to advanced capture tools, higher resolution processing, and expanded export formats to bring your creative projects to life.

Business Plan Designed for teams and businesses who need to document, measure, and collaborate on projects efficiently. This tier includes team collaboration features, measurement tools, floor plan generation, and sharing capabilities. Perfect for construction teams, architects, facility managers, and project professionals who need everyone capturing and sharing documentation seamlessly..

Enterprise Plan Our Enterprise tier is for organizations deploying 3D capture at scale across teams and locations. You'll get dedicated account support, enterprise-grade security and compliance, custom integrations with your existing tools, volume pricing, and advanced administrative controls to manage users and projects across your organization.

Do you offer a free trial?

Free trials are exclusively available with Basic or Business annual plans, where you can test the complete service for 7 days. During the trial, you'll have complete access to all the features of your chosen plan. You can cancel anytime during the trial period to avoid being charged. After your trial period, if you choose not to cancel, you will be automatically enrolled in an annual plan. Our Enterprise plan doesn't offer a trial, but you can contact our sales team for a demo.

Can I try Polycam before committing to a monthly subscription?

Free trials are exclusively available with Basic and Business annual plans, where you can test the complete service for 7 days. Monthly plans require payment upfront with no trial period.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan, or switch between yearly and monthly?

How to change your plan depends on where you originally subscribed:


Subscribed through Polycam website:

Contact Polycam support by clicking this link
Subscribed through App Store (Apple) or Google Play:

Manage subscription through your Apple ID / Google Play Store settings
Must cancel current plan before switching
Changes take effect at next renewal date
What if I decide to cancel?
You can cancel your subscription at any time through the app. However, yearly subscriptions will remain active until the renewal date, as we do not offer prorated refunds.
Do you offer any discounts?

Yes!


Sign up for a free polycam account to subscribe to our mailing list and receive updates about special promotions and discounts.
You can also save by choosing an annual plan, which provides a significant discount compared to monthly billing.
What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) for individual subscriptions. For Enterprise plans, we also offer invoice-based payment options.

Can you charge me in my local currency?
Polycam prices are billed in USD. Your bank or credit card provider handles the currency conversion automatically. The exact amount will be calculated using the exchange rate at the time of purchase.
Can I pay via invoice or wire transfer?

Invoice and wire transfer payments are available for Enterprise clients. All other subscriptions require payment by credit card. Please contact our sales team to learn more about Enterprise payment options.

Is there an option for a one-time payment to own the software?
No, Polycam is only available through subscription plans. We don't offer a one-time purchase option. Our subscription model allows us to continuously improve the platform, release new features, and provide regular updates to our users.
