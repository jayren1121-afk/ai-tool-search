Frequently Asked Questions
What is Moises?

Moises is the creative suite for musicians. We offer technology to help you practice, perform, and produce music, with tools such as Stem Separation, Chord Finder, Speed Changer, Lyric Transcription, and much more. Moises has been recognized as a Finalist for the 2025 Apple Design Awards, a Winner of the 2024 iPad App of the Year, and a Winner of the 2025 Microsoft Store Awards. Our tools are trusted by Grammy-winning artists, prestigious institutions such as the Berklee College of Music, and 70 million musicians across the world.

Will Moises ever train on my data?

No. Moises will never train on your data. Read our terms of service for more details.

How does Moises train its models?

Moises only trains our models on licensed materials. We never train on any music without the owner’s full approval. It’s important to us that musicians and other rights holders are paid for their work.

Is Moises free?

Moises offers a free tier that provides a limited number of uploads each month, with access to a select set of features. For full functionality and unlimited uploads, you will need to upgrade to a paid subscription. Log in to see our full pricing and features table.

Is Moises available online, or do I need to download an app?

Moises offers a web app that runs directly in your browser, as well as applications for Desktop, iPhone, iPad, and Android.
