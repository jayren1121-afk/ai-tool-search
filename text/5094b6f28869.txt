Frequently asked questions
Can I try the tools before subscribing?
Yes. New users get 100 free credits to test basic generation. Some advanced tools still require a paid plan.
How does the free credit system work?
New users receive a one-time balance of 100 free credits. These do not refresh daily or monthly.
Where can I see the price of my subscription?
You can view your current plan and pricing anytime in your Billing settings. Click on your profile and then select Manage My Subscription.
What payment methods can I use?
We accept major credit and debit cards. Depending on your location and device, additional payment methods may also be available at checkout.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my subscription?
Yes. You can change your plan at any time. If you upgrade, you only pay the extra amount for the rest of your current billing period.
Can I cancel my plan anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your plan at any time from your profile by clicking Manage My Subscription. You'll still have access until the end of your monthly billing period.
Can I get a receipt for my subscription?
Yes, you can access your receipts and invoices from your profile under Manage My Subscription.
How many credits does each tool use?

Credits reset monthly. Different tools consume different amounts of credits based on the computing power required.

Do my monthly credits roll over?
No, credits reset at the start of each billing cycle.
Can I buy more credits?
Yes, you can buy more credits under your profile.
I have credits. Why does the app still ask me to upgrade?
Some tools are only available on paid plans even if you still have credits. Credits cover usage, but feature access depends on your plan tier. If the tool is plan-restricted, you'll still see the upgrade screen.
Are my uploaded files private?
All uploads are private and not shared or used for training, they are only accessible to you. All generated patterns are private for Pro and Business accounts. On Standard accounts, selected designs generated from text may appear in the public gallery.
I have a question. How do I contact support?
For any additional questions, contact support at support@patterned.ai.
