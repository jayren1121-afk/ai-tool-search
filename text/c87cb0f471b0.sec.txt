How and when are credits consumed?
---
Credits are used when something is generated, never while you're editing or previewing. What each product uses:
---
Dubbing - per minute of video dubbed: 80 credits with lip-sync, 40 without. Your 10 included minutes each month can be used for dubbing as well as video creation.
Surveys - 350 credits per response, after the 3 responses included every month. Building and publishing a survey is free.
B-roll - clips and images you generate in the AI Playground or Media Library are charged per asset; the cost depends on the model and is shown before you generate.
Interactive Avatars (API) - per minute of conversation: 10 credits for the avatar only, 20 with the full widget.
