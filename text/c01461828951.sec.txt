How much does AI2SQL cost?
---
Start is $9 per month, Pro is $19 per month and Team is $39 per month. Billed annually, they are $7, $14 and $29 per month (save 25%). There is also a one-time Lifetime plan for $29.
---
Is there a free plan?
---
Yes. The Free plan includes 3 SQL queries a day on a demo database, and you can connect one database of your own and get 3 answers from it. Paid plans add more queries, SQL explanations and fixes; Pro and Team keep querying your own databas
---
How does the free trial work?
---
Every paid plan starts with a 7-day trial with full access to the plan you pick. A credit card is required to start the trial, and $0 is charged today. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you pay nothing.
