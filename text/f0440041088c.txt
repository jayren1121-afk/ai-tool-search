Frequently Asked Questions
What is AlphaSense?

AlphaSense is the AI platform redefining market intelligence for business and finance. With the world’s largest content library that includes premium, proprietary, public, and private sources, as well as AI technology that extracts key insights and delivers analysis-ready outputs, AlphaSense helps you make smarter business decisions, expedite your research workflows, and get the competitive edge.

Founded in 2011 by Jack Kokko (CEO) and Raj Neervannan (CTO), AlphaSense was built to solve a problem Kokko experienced firsthand as an investment banker at Morgan Stanley: the inefficiency of manual research. Today, AlphaSense serves over 7,000+ enterprise customers, including 90% of the S&P 100, 90% of the top investment banks, and 95% of the top consultancies.

What Data and Content Sets are Available in AlphaSense?

AlphaSense aggregates over 500+ million documents from premium private and public sources. This includes:

Regulatory filings, including SEC and global filings, earning call transcripts, and more.
Public and private company data, including financial information, employee data, company documents, and peer comparisons.
Company presentations and disclosures, including event transcripts, ESG reports, company presentations, press releases, and more.
Global equity research from top tier banking and independent research teams.
Broad range of financial and business sources, including news, trade journals, and other media sources.
Life sciences content covering clinical trials, biomedical research, global public health information, and more.
Expert call transcripts between industry operators and buy-side analysts
And much more.

Organizations can also integrate their own internal documents through Enterprise Intelligence for federated search across proprietary content.

Who Uses AlphaSense?

AlphaSense serves 7,000+ enterprise customers across financial services (investment banking, private equity, hedge funds, asset management, venture capital), corporations (life sciences, technology, energy, industrials, consumer goods), and professional services (consulting, law firms, insurance). Additionally, 90% of S&P 100 companies use the platform.

Functions commonly served include: competitive intelligence, market intelligence, corporate strategy, business strategy, corporate finance, business intelligence, investor relations, corporate development, M&A, investment research, consulting, and more.

How Much Does AlphaSense Cost?

We provide annual subscriptions for all team sizes, ranging from enterprise packages to per-seat options. Please reach out, and a member of our team will be in touch to discuss pricing.

Is AlphaSense Secure for Enterprise Use?

Yes. AlphaSense is SOC 2 Type II and ISO/IEC 27001 certified, with data encrypted at rest and in transit and every customer provisioned a dedicated, encrypted storage environment. For organizations using Enterprise Intelligence to search internal documents, additional options apply: bring your own key (BYOK) and bring your own bucket (BYOB) deployment models, plus permission mirroring so existing access controls carry over automatically. With BYOB, documents can live entirely within your own AWS environment.

Does AlphaSense Have a Mobile App?

Yes, AlphaSense offers a mobile app for both iOS and Android. Through the app, you can access AI-driven insights, get instant answers to research questions via Generative Search, execute agentic workflows via Workflow Agents and Custom Workflow Agents, and keep your momentum going even when you are away from your desk.
