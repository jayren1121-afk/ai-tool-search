Machine Setup

Comfy Cloud runs on Blackwell RTX 6000 Pro – 96GB VRAM

Time limit per job

Each workflow run has a maximum duration of 30 minutes. On the Pro plan, the time limit is increased to 1 hour. Jobs exceeding that limit are automatically cancelled to ensure fair usage and system stability.

Usage

You're only charged for active GPU time while a workflow is running. Idle time (e.g. time spent building workflows) does not consume GPU hours.

Credit balance

All plans will include a monthly pool of credits that are spent on active workflow runtime and Partner Nodes like Nano Banana Pro.

Add more credits anytime

Purchase additional credits at any time. Unused top-ups roll over to the next month automatically for up to 1 year.

Pre-installed models

Access a library of 900+ pre-installed models.

Custom nodes support

Comfy Cloud currently supports a variety of the most-used custom nodes from the ComfyUI community. We're expanding support regularly based on demand and compatibility.

Partner Nodes

Run proprietary models through Comfy's Partner Nodes, such as Nano Banana. The amount of credits each node uses depends on the model and parameters you set in the node, but these credits are the same ones that your monthly subscription comes with. These credits can also be used across Comfy Cloud and local ComfyUI. Read more about Partner nodes here.

Job queue

Queue up to 100 workflows at once.

Custom LoRA importing

For those on the Creator, Pro, or Team plans, you can bring in your own models & LoRAs from CivitAI or Huggingface to perfect your own style.

Parallel job execution

Run multiple workflows in parallel to speed up your pipeline.

Comfy Agent

Build and run workflows by describing what you want. Comfy Agent works on every plan, and new users get free credits to try it.

Available on every plan

Any Comfy Cloud account can use Comfy Agent. It is not locked to a specific plan: all you need is free new-user credits or a positive credit balance.

Billed in Comfy Credits

Once your free credits are used up, Comfy Agent draws on your Comfy Credits for LLM tokens, at the same rates as LLM Partner Nodes. On the Team plan, usage comes from the shared credit pool.

Workflow runs

Workflows the agent runs are charged exactly like runs you start yourself, with no surcharge, and follow your plan's runtime and job limits.

No message cap

There is no limit on how many messages you send. Past your plan's limits, new tasks wait in line instead of failing. When your credits run out, the agent pauses until you add more.

Model

Comfy picks the model for you. Bringing your own LLM key is not supported yet.

Comfy API pricing

Comfy API lets you deploy your workflow as a production API. GPU time and storage are billed by the second; build, deployment, and concurrency limits depend on your plan.
