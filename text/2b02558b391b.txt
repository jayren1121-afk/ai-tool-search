Text to image and image to image
*
Pricing is per image generated. Charges are based on the resolution tier selected in the request, regardless of the resolution at which the image is processed internally, and apply equally to text-to-image and image-to-image generations.
*
Reference images, whether provided by the user or retrieved by the model, and prompts are included at no extra charge. The per-image rate does not vary with the number of reference images provided, up to the maximum set out in the documentation.
*
The resolution tier is selected in the request; all supported aspect ratios within a tier are charged the same. Delivered dimensions may differ slightly from the nominal size (dimensions are rounded to multiples of 16). Supported resolutions, aspect ratios and reference image limits are set out in the documentation.
*
Reference images up to 16 MP are accepted (larger ones are rejected); images above 4 MP are downscaled to 4 MP before processing. Rejected requests, failed generations and outputs withheld by moderation are not charged.
*
Tiers denote the approximate pixel count of the output: 768²≈0.6 MP, 1K≈1 MP (1024×1024-equivalent), 1.5K≈2 MP, 2K≈4 MP, 4K≈16 MP.
