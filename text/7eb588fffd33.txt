Frequently asked questions
Can I use Kittl for free?

Yes — the free plan is your creative starting point.

You get access to curated templates and tools — perfect for trying things out, exploring the editor, learning to design like a pro, or making passion projects.

Ready to sell your designs, work with clients, or scale campaigns? That’s where paid plans come in.

Why are some templates only available on paid plans?

Because they support real designers — just like you.

Kittl’s 10,000+ Premium Templates and Mockups are created by world-class independent designers.

When you subscribe, a portion of your payment goes directly to these artists — helping sustain their work and grow the design community.

On free plans, you can explore a hand-picked selection, but upgrading unlocks the full library and supports the creatives behind it.

What’s included in paid plans?

Everything you need to work faster, smarter, and more professionally.

With a paid plan, you unlock:

- Full access to premium templates and assets

- High-resolution and vector exports

- More AI tokens and features like Kittl Flows

- Bigger project limits and commercial licensing




If you sell products, run a brand, or deliver creative work — this is where Kittl becomes a true creative engine. For a detailed overview, have a look on the table above to compare features per plan.

Will my subscription renew automatically?

Yes — but you’re always in control. Plans renew monthly or annually, depending on what you choose.

Want to use Kittl for just one project or for a short time only? Subscribe and you can always cancel right away when you don’t need it anymore — you’ll still have full access for the full billing period.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — no strings attached.

Cancel directly from your account settings at any time. Your access continues until the end of the billing cycle.

Can I invite teammates to my Kittl workspace?

Yes — team collaboration is built in so you can easily invite others.

Invite others to co-create, edit, and manage designs together in one shared space. Perfect for brand teams, agencies, or creative partners. Viewers are always free. Additional editors will be charged according to the plan you choose.

Can I use Kittl designs commercially?

Yes — with the right plan, you’re fully covered.

- Free Plan: For personal use only (no commercial rights).

- Pro & Expert Plans: Come with a Commercial License — reproduce your designs in up to 500,000 copies across packaging, marketing, digital media, or print.

- Business Plans: Include an Enhanced License for wider reach beyond the 500k limit.




Whether you're launching a product line, building a brand, or delivering client work — we've got you covered. For full licensing details read here → Link.

What are tokens and how do they work?

Tokens power Kittl’s AI features.

Use them to:

- Generate stunning visuals with AI

- Create product backgrounds

- Run smart design flows




Each AI action consumes a few tokens. Your plan includes a monthly set of tokens to fuel your creativity.

Do unused tokens roll over?

No. Your monthly tokens arrive at the start of each billing cycle and are yours to use any time that month. Whatever's left when the cycle ends doesn't carry into the next one, and your balance starts fresh.

If you're running out before the month is up, you can upgrade your plan or top up if you are in Expert or Max plan.

How do daily tokens work?

On Expert and Max you get a batch of tokens every day, on top of your monthly balance. Expert gets 200 a day, Max gets 600. Daily tokens reset every 24 hours. Your daily tokens are used first before your monthly balance.

Can I try premium features before upgrading?

Yes — we want you to explore first. Free users can preview most Pro features with light usage limits. You’ll know exactly what you’re unlocking before committing to a paid plan.

What happens to my designs if I cancel?

Your work is always safe.

Canceling your subscription does not delete your designs. You’ll still have access to your projects, though premium features like vector exports will be locked until you reactivate.

I’m a solo creator — is it worth upgrading?

Yes — especially if you value speed, polish, and control. Kittl is the fastest way to turn creative ideas into professional designs, combining instant inspiration with intuitive tools that make designing feel effortless.

Upgrading means:

- Faster workflows

- Smarter variations

- Commercial-ready assets

- Features like AI & Flows that help you scale




For one monthly price, you get access to first class content that saves hundreds of dollars, and tools that save hours, and help you create like a full design team.
