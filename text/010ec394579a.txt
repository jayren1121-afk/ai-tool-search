Frequently asked questions
Can I use the generated voice commercially?

Yes. All paid Listnr plans (Individual, Solo, and Agency) include commercial rights for the audio you generate, including monetized YouTube videos, ads, audiobooks, and presentations.

What happens when I run out of my monthly quota?

If you run out of credits, you cannot generate new audio until the quota resets at the next billing cycle. You can purchase extra credits in Listnr Studio or upgrade to a higher plan to continue immediately.

What is the difference between monthly and yearly billing?

Yearly billing charges 10 months up front — two months free compared with paying month to month. Monthly billing renews each month at the listed rate.

What happens to my quota when I upgrade or downgrade?

Upgrades typically apply the new quota immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and PayPal. Enterprise plans can use invoicing and wire transfers.

How can I upgrade my plan?

Upgrade any time from Subscription or Billing in Listnr Studio. The prorated difference is calculated automatically.

How can I downgrade to the free tier?

Cancel the paid subscription from your dashboard. The account reverts to the free tier at the end of the current paid period.

What is your refund policy?

We generally do not refund partial months or used quota. If a technical issue blocks the service, contact support in the first few days after purchase.

Where can I find my invoice?

Invoices are emailed automatically. You can also download past invoices from Billing history in Listnr Studio.
