Frequently Asked Questions
Is there a free version of Coursebox available?

Coursebox includes a free plan that allows users to start building courses and exploring the core AI features at no cost, with unlimited learners and no credit card required to begin.

Does Coursebox offer an enterprise plan?

Yes. Enterprise is our contract channel, built for universities, government and large organisations. It is sold under a signed agreement with a minimum 2-year initial term, and it is the only plan that includes MSAs, DPAs, SLAs, vendor security assessments, single sign-on and a custom hosting location. Every other plan is self-serve: bought online by card under our standard published terms. To explore Enterprise, book a demo or email support@coursebox.ai.

Are there limits on learners with Coursebox plans?

Coursebox allows unlimited learners on all plans, including the free plan, allowing you to train small teams or large organisations without seat-based fees.

How does Coursebox price AI usage?

Coursebox plans include a set number of AI credits or generated text, images, and videos per month or year, with higher tiers providing larger allowances for heavier usage.

Can I cancel my plan at any time?

Yes, on self-serve plans. You can cancel anytime from your account settings in a couple of clicks, with no lock-in contracts. Cancelling stops future billing and your account stays active, so your courses are not deleted. If you want to try Coursebox risk free first, start on the free plan, no credit card required. Enterprise is different: it is sold under a signed agreement with a minimum 2-year initial term.
