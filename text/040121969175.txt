Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Liner for free?
Yes, but a free account offers limited credits which may run out quickly with just a few tasks. You can also access latest AI models, upload more files, search ad-free, and more when you upgrade.
What payment methods do you accept?
Liner accepts major credit cards. For more information, please contact us.
Do you offer refunds?
We do not offer refunds but you can cancel your subscription before it automatically renews or before your free trial ends. To cancel your subscription, please visit Settings - Subscription.
How do I cancel my paid plan?
If you paid with card, please visit the settings page on our website to cancel your plan. Subscription will be automatically started or renewed until you cancel the plan.
If you paid but were not upgraded.
Upgrade can take time depending on the network condition. Please wait 5-10 minutes or refresh the page. If your account is still not upgraded, please contact us.
