FAQs
Is Reclaim for individuals, teams, or entire companies?

We designed Reclaim’s pricing and product experience to work fantastically for individuals, but Reclaim gets more and more powerful as you scale it out to your teams and company. When the whole company uses Reclaim to intelligently schedule their meetings and heads-down time, everyone’s calendars become more optimized and flexible, which unlocks increased time savings and productivity.

Oftentimes, we see individuals or teams initially upgrade to use Reclaim across their calendars, and then advocate for upgrading the entire company.

If you’re a team looking to roll Reclaim out, or if you have questions about how to advocate for upgrading your company, book time with us here.

How does Reclaim's pricing work?

The free Lite plan is available for any single user at no cost. Our paid plans are tiered by team size, though you can upgrade from Starter to Business to unlock additional features for your account. We offer a 29% discount for annual billing vs. monthly.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards. Contact us at hello@reclaim.ai to discuss additional payment methods. Some payment methods may require a minimum purchase.

Can I get a demo?

Absolutely! We hold demo webinars every other week. Join us for our next one! If you’d like a demo for your entire team, send us a note through our contact form.

Who do I contact to complete a security assessment?

Reach out through our contact form, or send us a note at security@reclaim.ai, and our security team will happily assist with all of your security requirements.
