Questions about pricing
What is a credit, and how many will I need?
One page is one credit. Ten rows of data is one credit. You pay whichever is more, so a list page with 50 products costs 5 credits, and a page that returns two fields still costs 1. Every run shows its cost in your dashboard before it goes.
